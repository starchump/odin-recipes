Marinara Sauce

Description

Simple, tangy, savory tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Olive oil
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Canned diced tomatos
  • Canned crushed tomatos
  • Salt
  • Fresh basil

Steps

  1. Sautee diced onions
  2. Add minced garlic and sautee until fragrant
  3. Add both cans of tomatos and simmer to desired consistency
  4. Add sprig of fresh basil near end of cooking
  5. Salt to taste
